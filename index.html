A short introduction to the most exciting new GCSE in a generation

Grades open the door. A portfolio a student can defend in person is what gets them noticed by an admissions panel.

Why grades alone aren’t enough any more

Admissions boards see thousands of similar applications every year, many built around exam grades alone. GPAs have been climbing across many school systems for years, which makes a strong set of grades harder to use as a point of difference.

The Global Skills Projects gives students that something else: an Individual Project and a Group Sustainability Action Project they can point to directly.

Assessed by doing, not by sitting an exam

Two pieces of real project work over two years. Both are formally graded and certified by OxfordAQA, the international arm of AQA, one of the UK’s major exam boards.

Year 1

Individual Project

Students choose their own topic and develop it as a written report of around 2,500 words, or as a practical project with a shorter supporting report.

60%of the final grade
Year 2

Group Sustainability Action Project

Students work in teams on a real sustainability challenge, then present their findings formally.

40%of the final grade
